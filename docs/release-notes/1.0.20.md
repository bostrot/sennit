- **Quick messages (Pro).** Tap + in a chat and pick Happy Birthday, Thank you, Running late and more: your AI writes a short message that fits the conversation, in its language, and puts it in the composer for you to check and send. Add your own under Settings → AI → Quick messages.
- **Link previews.** Links now show a card with the page's title and image. They're on by default in chats that aren't end-to-end encrypted; encrypted chats have their own switch, off by default, under Settings → Link previews. Previews sent by WhatsApp, Signal and Telegram show up too.

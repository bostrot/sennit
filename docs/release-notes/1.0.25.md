- **AI reply suggestions show up with self-hosted models.** Notifications no longer wait for the suggestions: they appear at once, and the suggestions are added a moment later without a second buzz. Thinking models are asked to skip their reasoning for these two short answers, which makes them several times faster. You can also pick a separate, faster model for suggestions under Settings → Endpoints.
- **Personal memory keeps itself up to date.** With automatic learning on, Sennit rereads an active chat after about 30 new messages or once a day while the app is open, and updates what it remembers by itself. Each learned fact shows when it was learned; edit, pin or delete it, or undo the last update. Every AI feature now uses the memory, to-dos and calendar titles included. The settings text is shorter, with details behind "Learn more".

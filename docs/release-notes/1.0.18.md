- **Connect your messengers in a few taps.** Settings → Messengers walks you through WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and more step by step: no bot chat, no commands.
- **Reply reminders keep going until you answer.** A chat you read and didn't answer comes back until you reply, tap Stop reminding or exclude it, even with Sennit closed.
- **Better calendar titles.** When you add a date from a chat to your calendar, the title now comes from the whole conversation — both sides — so it reads "Dinner with Anna at Luigi's", not "Meeting".
- **Smoother typing in busy groups.** Others typing or reading along no longer makes the composer lag.
- **New messengers show up sooner.** When your server adds one, Sennit offers it within the hour instead of days later.

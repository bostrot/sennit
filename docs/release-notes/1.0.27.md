### Messages
- **React with any emoji.** The "+" next to the quick reactions opens every emoji your phone can show, with groups, search, skin tones and recents.
- **Save messages** to find them again, synced end-to-end encrypted to your other devices.
- **Translate a message** with your own AI model: long-press it and choose Translate.
- **Hide a message for yourself**, and optionally hide "Message deleted" rows.
- **Mark unread from here**: long-press a message to make the chat unread from that point on.
- **Polls**: vote, change your vote, see who voted and create your own. WhatsApp and Signal polls now show as polls.
- **Jump to a date**: tap the date between messages and pick a day.
- **Links everywhere.** Web and email addresses and phone numbers are tappable in bridged and SMS messages; hold a link to copy or share it. WhatsApp business messages show their buttons as buttons.
- **Join Matrix rooms** from matrix.to links.

### Writing and sending
- **Drafts stay put** per chat, show as "Draft:" in the inbox and sync to your other devices.
- **Messages wait for the connection**: sent offline, they go out by themselves once you're back online.
- **Send one message to several chats** at once.
- **Long messages arrive whole** on networks with a length limit, like Telegram, split at natural breaks.
- **Photo quality**: original, high or data saver; photos too large for a network shrink to fit.
- **Voice messages**: pause while recording, play at 1.5× or 2×.
- **Emoji by name on desktop**: type `:thumbs` and pick from the list.

### Chats and contacts
- **Needs reply and Drafts filters** in the inbox, and the archive per network. A reaction can count as a reply.
- **Private nickname and photo** for any chat, only for you.
- **Names instead of numbers** in SMS conversations, from your contacts.
- **Open a contact in its own app**: WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and more.
- Tap a contact's profile picture to see it full size.
- **One memory per person**: a one-to-one chat has a single "About" memory, and temporary facts like appointments expire after their date.

### Notifications
- **Know when someone reacts** to your message, separately for direct chats and groups.
- **A sound for each chat**, or silence without muting.
- **Remind me later** about a chat right from its notification.
- **Everything scheduled in one list**: scheduled messages and reminders, editable.

### Sennit Web
- Copy and paste work again, also over the local network.
- A sent message no longer stays on the clock after it has gone out; failed sends show why and can be retried.

### Settings and privacy
- **App lock** with a PIN or biometrics; chats stay hidden in the recent-apps view.
- **Text size**: make Sennit's text, or just the messages, bigger or smaller.
- **Search settings** by name or description.
- **Storage**: see and limit how much space media takes, and load photos only on tap.
- **Anonymous usage statistics**, only if you allow it: Sennit asks once and stays off until you say yes.
- After signing in, verifying your device comes first again, before the notification question.
- File sizes use your language's decimal comma.
- Sennit may ask for a store rating once you've used it a while, at most once per version.

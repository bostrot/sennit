- **Patience with local AI models.** A model running on your own computer or server (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, a litellm proxy) now gets up to 10 minutes for quick messages, auto-replies and to-dos instead of giving up after a minute or less. Hosted APIs like OpenAI keep their short limits. While a quick message is being written, the bar shows how long it has been, and Cancel really stops the request.
- **Memory for chats and people.** Every chat, groups included, now has its own notes (topics, plans, the tone you use there), and every person a short one used in all their chats (nickname, birthday, du or Sie). Turn on **Settings → AI → Personal memory** and quick messages, auto-replies and reply suggestions use them. "Learn from chat" suggests facts you accept, add to someone's profile, or dismiss. Notes stay on your device.

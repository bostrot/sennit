- **Sennit in your browser.** Turn on Settings → Sennit Web on your phone, scan the QR code with your computer, and your chats open in its browser — the same app, served by your phone over your network and end-to-end encrypted. Nothing about the browser is stored on a server, and you can unpair a browser at any time.
- **Sennit in your browser, from anywhere.** Run a small relay on your own Cloudflare account and your phone serves Sennit Web to your paired browsers wherever they are. The relay only passes encrypted traffic along. See the setup guide under Settings → Sennit Web.

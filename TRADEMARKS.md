# Trademarks

Sennit's code is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The licence
covers the code. It does not cover the name or the look.

"Sennit", "Sennit Messenger", "Sennit Pro" and the plaited-strand icon identify
the builds that Eric Trenkel publishes: the ones on this repository's release
page, on Google Play and on the App Store.

## What you can do without asking

- Build Sennit from source and use it, under its own name, for yourself.
- Say that your project is a fork of Sennit, is based on Sennit, or works with
  Sennit.
- Package an unmodified Sennit for a distribution, under its own name.

## What needs a different name

If you publish a modified Sennit — on a store, a website or anywhere other
people get it — give it your own name and your own icon, and use your own
application id. People who install it should not have to wonder who built it
or who to ask when it breaks. A fork that sells a paid tier must not call it
"Sennit Pro".
